# IEEE Conference Paper Formatting Guidelines

## General Layout
*   **Paper Size**: US Letter (8.5" x 11") or A4.
*   **Orientation**: Portrait.
*   **Columns**: Two-column format.
    *   Column width: 3.5 inches (88.9 mm).
    *   Space between columns: 0.25 inches (6.35 mm).
*   **Margins**:
    *   Top: 0.75" (19 mm).
    *   Bottom: 1" (25.4 mm).
    *   Left/Right: 0.625" (15.9 mm).
*   **Font**: Times New Roman.
    *   Title: 24pt, Regular.
    *   Author Names: 11pt, Regular.
    *   Section Headings: 10pt, Small Caps, Centered (Level 1).
    *   Body Text: 10pt, Regular.
*   **Line Spacing**: Single spaced.

## Structure & Headings
*   **Level 1 Heading**: Centered, Small Caps, Roman Numerals (e.g., I. INTRODUCTION).
*   **Level 2 Heading**: Left-aligned, Italic, Letters (e.g., *A. Dataset Overview*).
*   **Level 3 Heading**: Indented, Italic, Numbers (e.g., *1) Preprocessing:*).

## Figures and Tables
*   **Position**: Top or bottom of columns (avoid middle). Large figures can span both columns.
*   **Captions**:
    *   Figures: "Fig. 1. Description..." (Below the figure).
    *   Tables: "TABLE I. DESCRIPTION..." (Above the table).
*   **Resolution**: High quality (300dpi+).

## Citation & References
*   **In-text Citation**: Square brackets, e.g., [1], [2], [3]-[5].
*   **Reference List**: Numbered in order of appearance.
*   **Format Examples**:
    *   *Journal*: [1] A. Author, "Title of paper," *Abbrev. Title of Journal*, vol. x, no. x, pp. xxx-xxx, Abbrev. Month, year.
    *   *Conference*: [2] A. Author, "Title of paper," in *Abbrev. Name of Conf.*, City of Conf., Abbrev. State (if given), year, pp. xxx-xxx.
    *   *Book*: [3] A. Author, *Title of Book*, xth ed. City of Publisher, Country: Publisher, year.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid
*   Do not use "I" or "We" excessively; prefer passive voice or "This paper proposes...".
*   Define acronyms on first use.
*   Equations should be numbered sequentially: (1), (2).
